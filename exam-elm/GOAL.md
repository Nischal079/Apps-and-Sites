## Brand & positioning
**ExamElm** — "Study smarter. Prepare better. Achieve more."
A free academic productivity toolkit for high-school and university students **worldwide**. Mission: help students organize their academic lives, prepare for exams, and track progress with simple, useful tools.

**Global-first, explicitly.** Not restricted to Nepal, India, or any single education board. Region-configurable from day one: US, UK, Indian boards, Nepal NEB, Singapore (NTU/NUS), more by demand. Never assume a grading formula — every regional configuration is documented and tested.

## The four tools (v1)
1. Study Timetable Generator
2. Grade Calculator
3. Syllabus Coverage Tracker
4. Exam Countdown Planner

## Core product principles
Free core · no mandatory registration · mobile-first · fast loading · clean modern UI · privacy-conscious (data stays in the browser) · save progress locally · configurable for education systems · accessible · no unnecessary complexity.
