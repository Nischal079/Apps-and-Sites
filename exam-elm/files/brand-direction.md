## Verdict: ⛔ BLOCKED — do not launch as "StudyPilot"

The name is in active commercial use by multiple education products. Proceeding risks user confusion, app-store rejection, and trademark disputes. Rename cost is near-zero pre-launch — do it now.

### Evidence of conflict

**Existing products/services using the exact name:**
- **"studypilot" Android app** (app.studypilot.mobile) by studypilot UG, Germany — Education category, updated Sep 12, 2026 (Google Play).
- **StudyPilot LTD** (studypilot.co.nz) — NZ eLearning aviation platform, operating with terms since 2021.
- **studypilot.com.au** — Australian tutoring service.
- **studypilot.co.uk** — UK university pathways service ("1,000+ applicants guided annually").
- **QuillGlow's flagship "StudyPilot" AI study feature** — 19k+ students, paid tiers.
- **"Study Pilot" iOS app series** by Coastal Bytes.
- **studypilotapp.netlify.app** — study app with a paid Pro tier (₦2,500/mo).
- **A funded McGill TechAccel Fall 2026 venture** named StudyPilot.

**Domain:** studypilot.com is **REGISTERED** (since 2015-12-26, expires 2027-12-26, GoDaddy, parked on Namebright DNS) — verified via RDAP, read-only.

Recommend a manual check on the chosen replacement name before launch.

### .com.np feasibility (for the replacement name)
- Registration is **FREE** via register.com.np (Mercantile).
- **Personal domains must match the applicant's name on their citizenship certificate** — "studypilot.com.np" (or any brand name) would likely be **rejected** for a personal application.
- The business route requires a **company registration certificate**.
- **Feasible only** under Nischal's own name (if the domain matches it) or after registering a business entity. Plan: launch on the free Cloudflare Pages subdomain; pursue .com.np later if/when the paperwork makes sense.
