# Keyword Demand + Free-Tool Opportunity Research
**Project:** Student Success Hub — Phase 1 market validation
**Date:** 2026-11-04
**Researcher:** research subagent
**Method:** free signals only — Google search results (page-1 composition per query), page text of top competitor tools, Reddit/community mentions via search snippets, affiliate/review roundups. No paid SEO tools, no accounts, no invented numbers.

## Honesty & signal legend
- **Demand: STRONG / MEDIUM / WEAK** = qualitative estimate based on observable signals (number of dedicated tools/sites built for the query, forum/roundup mentions, explicit "most searched" claims on ranking pages). Never a search-volume number — I have no access to one.
- **Evidence tags:** `[observed]` = seen in a search result or page I opened; `[estimate]` = my inference from those observations; `[claimed-by-page]` = a claim made by a ranking page itself (may be marketing).
- **Google Trends:** attempted 2026-11-04 via trends.google.com — blocked by upstream rate limit (HTTP 429). No Trends data. **Verdicts below rest on page-1 composition and qualitative signals only.**
- **Reddit:** direct `site:reddit.com` queries returned no results through this search interface. Reddit/community demand evidence comes from `[observed]` mentions inside ranking pages (e.g. wholesyllabus.com roundup citing r/college threads) — labeled as such.

## B) Free-tool opportunity catalog (15 tools)

Competitor friction noted from pages actually opened/read on 2026-11-04 (`[observed]`), or from search-result page text (`[snippet]`). All competitor claims are their own marketing; treat as such.

### T1. Final grade calculator ("what do I need on my final?")
- **Problem:** "I have 84% and the final is worth 30% — what do I need for an A?" The most-searched grade question every finals season (per usagradecalculator.com `[claimed-by-page]`).
- **Existing free options:** https://gpacalc.app/final-grade-calculator/ · https://usagradecalculator.com/ · https://gpacalculator.net/final-grade-calculator/ · https://www.gradcalcolator.com/ · https://gradeweight.com/ · https://www.pearson.com/channels/calculators/test-grade-calculator
- **What's wrong:** most handle ONE class at a time; worksheet365/gradeweight are cluttered; none do a clean multi-course "all my finals" planner; several are ad-heavy calculator-farm pages `[snippet/estimate]`.
- **Differentiation:** multi-class finals planner (all courses in one view, each with current grade + final weight + target), one-tap shareable result link ("I need 96% — send to study group"), mobile-first single screen, exam-date urgency sorting. This is the #1 build priority.

### T2. CGPA ↔ percentage converter (South-Asia-first)
- **Problem:** CBSE says ×9.5, VTU says (CGPA−0.75)×10, Anna/MAKAUT ×10, Mumbai Univ (7.1×CGPA)+11, generic 4.0 scale (÷4×100) — students constantly use the wrong formula on job/grad-school forms `[observed]` (calcmeter.com, cgpacalculatoronline.net).
- **Existing free options:** https://cgpacalculatoronline.net/cgpa-to-percentage-calculator/ · https://gpacalculator.net/cgpa-to-percentage-calculator/ · https://calcmeter.com/blog/cgpa-to-percentage.html · https://bulkcalculator.com/academiccalculators/cgpa-to-percentage.html
- **What's wrong:** single-formula tools mislead students from other boards; best info is buried in blog tables, not in the tool itself `[observed]`.
- **Differentiation:** ONE converter with a board/university switcher (CBSE/AICTE default ×9.5, VTU, Anna, Mumbai Univ, SPPU, generic 10-pt, 4.0/5.0 scales), reverse mode (percentage→CGPA), and a "check your marksheet" warning. Built for India/Nepal/Bangladesh/Pakistan traffic — the Hub's natural audience.

### T3. Study timetable generator
- **Problem:** students stare at blank weekly grids; existing generators either need accounts (Scholarly: 1 lifetime free AI creation `[snippet]`) or are generic schedule grids.
- **Existing free options:** https://www.scholarics.com/study-schedule · https://flashcardbuddy.com/tools/study-schedule-maker · https://www.timetablecreator.net/ · https://timetablecraft.com/ · https://timemanagementtools.net/timetable-creator/
- **What's wrong:** Scholarly gates downloads/printing behind paid plan; most are manual drag-and-drop grids, not generators; none auto-insert spaced-repetition review blocks `[snippet]`.
- **Differentiation:** enter subjects + difficulty + hours/day → generated week with hard subjects first AND auto-placed 1/3/7/14-day review blocks (Flashcard Buddy proves the concept works), one-click print/PDF, no signup, exam-date aware.

### T4. Exam countdown planner (+ daily study hours)
- **Problem:** a date alone doesn't plan; students need "I have 42 days → 2.5 hrs/day" plus the ticking clock for motivation.
- **Existing free options:** https://clock7.com/exam-countdown/ (opened 2026-11-04 — the best in class `[observed]`) · https://studybuddytools.com/pomodoro-timer/ · https://pomodorome.com/ · https://www.themagiccodex.com/countdown-timer/
- **What's wrong:** Clock7 is excellent but exam-preset pages are IELTS/SAT/GCSE/GRE/GMAT/TOEFL/USMLE/NEET/MDCAT-centric; generic countdown sites lack the study-hours math; most lack shareable accountability links `[observed]`.
- **Differentiation:** match Clock7's feature set (countdown + daily hours + built-in pomodoro + shareable link + .ics) but for ANY exam including South-Asian boards (CBSE, NEB/Nepal, HSC), plus a per-subject hours split. Or: be the Clock7 for the rest of the world.

### T5. Pomodoro timer
- **Problem:** staying focused in 25-min blocks; students want tasks + stats without accounts.
- **Existing free options:** https://www.101planners.com/pomodoro-timer/ (opened 2026-11-04 — superb: tasks, distraction parking, reflection, ambient sounds, no signup, no ads `[observed]`) · https://pomodoro-timer.com/ · https://tomatock.com/ · https://timeoora.com/
- **What's wrong:** nothing much — this is the most solved problem on the list. 101planners sets a very high bar `[observed]`.
- **Differentiation:** don't compete head-on; build a *good-enough* timer and differentiate by integration — timer sessions auto-log into the study-hours tracker (T11) and syllabus tracker (T12). The moat is the ecosystem, not the timer.

### T6. Flashcard maker (printable + simple web decks)
- **Problem:** Anki is powerful but ugly/intimidating; Quizlet free tier has ads; students want quick printable cards or simple digital decks without accounts.
- **Existing free options:** https://emstudio.pro/tools/flashcard-maker (printable, no watermark) · https://www.themagiccodex.com/flashcard-maker/ (decks + spaced repetition, no signup) · https://www.flashcardsgenerator.com/ · https://tooleem.com/tools/flashcards-generator · https://flashcardmaker.me/free-flashcard-maker (1 deck/day, 10 cards free — gated `[snippet]`)
- **What's wrong:** AI flashcard tools gatekeep (flashcardmaker.me limits, Repeto/Adobe need accounts); Anki/Quizlet need apps/accounts; printable tools are bare-bones `[snippet]`.
- **Differentiation:** fastest path from "paste my notes list" → printable double-sided PDF with cut lines AND a simple flip-mode web deck with lightweight spaced repetition — all no-signup. Target the long-tail: "printable flashcards no watermark".

### T7. Revision schedule generator (spaced-repetition review planner)
- **Problem:** students plan *learning* but forget *reviewing*; the 1-3-7-14/21 day intervals are proven but nobody schedules them.
- **Existing free options:** https://flashcardbuddy.com/tools/study-schedule-maker (does 1/3/7/14-day reviews `[snippet]`) · https://gradelio.co.uk/tools/gcse-revision-timetable-generator (GCSE-only, UK)
- **What's wrong:** Flashcard Buddy's tool is a single-purpose page with dated UX; Gradelio is UK-GCSE-locked; nothing generic + beautiful `[snippet/estimate]`.
- **Differentiation:** exam-date-backwards planner: enter exam dates + topics + confidence (red/amber/green) → day-by-day plan with first-learn + spaced review passes, past-paper slots, buffer days; export to calendar. This is T3's smarter sibling — possibly merge T3+T7 into one "study planner" tool.

### T8. Cornell notes template generator (printable)
- **Problem:** students want the 3-zone layout (cue column ~30%, notes ~70%, summary strip) in A4/Letter, ruled/dotted/blank, without hunting template galleries.
- **Existing free options:** https://miniwebtool.com/cornell-notes-generator/ (generator, free, no signup) · https://graphpaperpdf.net/cornell-notes-template/ · https://www.101planners.com/cornell-notes-template/ · https://affine.pro/blog/the-cornell-notes-template-and-system-learning-tips
- **What's wrong:** miniwebtool's generator works but is bare; 101planners/AFFiNE are download galleries, not generators; Etsy charges $4.65 for packs `[observed]`.
- **Differentiation:** prettier generator with subject presets, lined/dotted/grid options, A4+Letter, multi-page, classroom-friendly print CSS — plus a one-page "how to use Cornell notes" guide on the same page for the SEO.

### T9. Essay planner (word-budget outline builder)
- **Problem:** "2500-word essay due Friday" → students need thesis → paragraphs → evidence → word split, not a blank page.
- **Existing free options:** https://www.hlper.co.uk/guides/essay-plan-template.html (free planner + PDF/Word, no signup `[snippet]`) · https://gradedraft.com/essay/essay-outline-generator/ (rule-based, no signup) · https://www.planory.ca/ (AI, free tier)
- **What's wrong:** AI planners are account/limit-gated; HLPER is UK-blog-styled and thin; none combine word-budget math + deadline phasing (research 25% → outline 10% → draft 40% → edit 15%) `[snippet/estimate]`.
- **Differentiation:** rule-based (no AI cost!) essay planner: question → thesis → argument/counter-argument → paragraph plan with per-section word budgets from total word count → phased deadline schedule. Printable one-pager. Zero AI, zero signup — the anti-ChatGPT essay tool (planning help, not writing).

### T10. Assignment deadline planner
- **Problem:** missed deadlines; LMS apps need accounts and don't cover everyone; students want a 60-second list with reminders.
- **Existing free options:** https://www.everythingabuad.com/p/student-assignment-tracker.html (free web tool, no signup, Google Calendar export `[snippet]` — the model to copy) · https://www.studyrows.com/ (free, paste-from-sheets) · apps: MyStudyLife, Coursicle, Power Planner, DormWay (all account-based)
- **What's wrong:** everything good is an app with signup; manual planners die from data-entry friction `[estimate]`.
- **Differentiation:** the everythingabuad.com formula for a global audience: add assignments with priority/type, deadline countdowns, weekly view, .ics export, browser notifications, all in localStorage — "the assignment tracker that needs no account."

### T11. Study session logger (hours per subject + streaks)
- **Problem:** "am I actually studying enough?" Students want logged hours per subject, weekly pace vs target, streaks — without an app install.
- **Existing free options:** https://www.mystudydiary.com/study-tracker (free PWA, no signup — strong `[snippet]`) · https://poroapp.com/ (app, free core) · Study Time Tracker app (620k installs, Android-only)
- **What's wrong:** best options are apps; web alternatives are thin; most track time OR tasks, not time-per-subject vs exam targets `[snippet/estimate]`.
- **Differentiation:** integrate with T4/T5: pomodoro sessions auto-log to subject totals; per-subject hour targets derived from exam countdown ("42 days × 2.5h ÷ 6 subjects"); weekly pace bars; streaks. As a standalone it's a MAYBE — as hub glue it's a must-have feature.

### T12. Syllabus coverage tracker (the blue-ocean bet)
- **Problem:** "how much of the syllabus have I actually finished?" — the #1 anxiety question with no good free web answer.
- **Existing free options:** apps only — StudyRide (studyride.in, India competitive exams), Ojee-Tracker (ojeet.tech, open-source), MindPrep AI (mindprepai.com, freemium), Poro app. **No strong global web tool** `[observed]`.
- **What's wrong:** app install required; India-exam-specific syllabi; premium gates on planning features `[snippet]`.
- **Differentiation:** web-first, any-syllabus tracker: paste/type chapters → check off not-started/in-progress/done → live completion % per subject → "on track / behind" vs exam date → spaced revision flags. Optionally ship with pre-loaded chapter lists for popular boards (CBSE, NEB Nepal). This is the highest-upside original tool on the list.

### T13. Formula sheet generator (printable one-pager)
- **Problem:** STEM students need all equations for a chapter on one printable page; existing sheets are static PDFs made for someone else's syllabus.
- **Existing free options:** https://scholarly.so/tools/formula-sheet-generator (AI, account + limits `[snippet]`) · https://formulasheet.com/db/ (database, dated UX) · static PDFs on Scribd/Docsity
- **What's wrong:** AI route needs signup and may hallucinate formulas; static PDFs aren't customizable `[estimate]`.
- **Differentiation:** curated, human-verified formula picker: choose subject (physics, chemistry, math, …) → tick the formulas you need → generates a clean printable one-pager with units + "when to use" notes. Trust (verified formulas) beats AI generation here. Start with 3 subjects, expand by demand.

### T14. Past-paper question bank organizer (personal)
- **Problem:** toppers swear by past papers, but students drown in PDFs; existing question banks are teacher tools.
- **Existing free options:** https://paperguides.org/ (Nepal, open-source past papers by topic — adjacent, not a personal organizer) · https://testpaper.io/ · https://questionforge.ai/ · https://practicequestions.akadesk.com/ (all teacher/institution-facing) `[observed]`
- **What's wrong:** nothing lets a *student* log "2023 Q4(b) — Laplace transform — attempted ✓ / got wrong ✗" and see topic-wise weak spots `[estimate]`.
- **Differentiation:** personal past-paper log: add questions by subject/topic/year/source → mark attempted + confidence → topic mastery dashboard (" Laplace: 12 attempted, 58% correct — weakest topic") → spaced re-attempt reminders. Pairs beautifully with the Hub's exam-prep guides. Long-term: crowdsourced topic-tagged banks per board (PaperGuides collaboration potential).

### T15. Semester planner template (printable, free, no email)
- **Problem:** back-to-school planning; Etsy charges $1–3, blogs gate downloads behind email.
- **Existing free options:** https://www.calendarpedia.com/academic-year-planner-pdf-templates.html · https://printgridcalendar.com/semester-planner/ · https://www.template.net/ (thin) · https://www.notion.com/templates/student-dashboard-696
- **What's wrong:** static PDFs can't adapt to YOUR semester dates; Notion templates need Notion + setup `[estimate]`.
- **Differentiation:** date-aware generator: enter semester start/end → get a printable semester-at-a-glance with YOUR weeks, deadline markers, exam weeks highlighted; A4/Letter; no email wall. The anti-Etsy: free forever.

## C) Underserved long-tail angles (18 queries, students' own phrasing)

Why these: each is a specific phrasing a student would type; page-1 for the head term is crowded, but these long-tails are served by thin pages, forums, or nothing. A new site wins them with one excellent purpose-built page/tool each. All `[estimate]` on winnability, grounded in the page-1 observations above.

1. **"what do i need on my final to get an A"** — Winnable because: ranking pages target the generic "final grade calculator"; a page answering this exact phrasing with a worked table (88% now, 20/25/30% weight → needed score) matches intent better. Pairs with T1.
2. **"cgpa to percentage calculator CBSE 9.5"** — Winnable because: board-specific intent; current rankers are generic converters. A CBSE-dedicated page + reverse mode wins. Pairs with T2.
