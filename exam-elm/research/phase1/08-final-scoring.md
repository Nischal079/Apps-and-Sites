# Final Candidate Scoring — Expanded Phase 1 Brief

**Date:** 2026-11-04
**Mandate:** Challenge the original "Student Success Hub" idea; full pivot allowed. Top 5 opportunities, one recommendation.
**Inputs:** 01 (30 problems), 01b (question themes), 02 (15 competitors), 03 (25 keywords, 15 tools), 04 (monetization + Nepal payments), 05 (first 5-niche scoring), 06 (Nepal/South-Asia exam-prep), 07 (3 alternative models: accountability, newsletter, creator brand).

**Scoring criteria** (the brief's priority order), 1–5 each, max 20:
- **(a) Real demand + realistic revenue path** (weighted first)
- **(b) Differentiation & quality** (over content quantity)
- **(c) Free interactive tools / recurring-visitor engine** (tool-led growth beats pure content)
- **(d) Minimal ongoing maintenance**

---

## The 9 candidates

### 1. Global free exam-prep toolkit hub — 20/20 ✅
*No-signup planners, grade calculators, exam trackers; South-Asia-friendly. (Original idea, challenged and re-tested.)*
- (a) 5 — demand converges from all streams (30 problems, #1 underserved need, 15 TARGET keywords); revenue verified day-one (AdSense Nepal wire) → affiliates → Paddle products, zero paperwork.
- (b) 5 — syllabus coverage tracker has no web incumbent; integrated tool ecosystem; no free all-in-one hub exists.
- (c) 5 — pure tool-led; shareable links = viral loop; tools compound without feeding.
- (d) 5 — evergreen tools; 0–1 hr/week steady state.
- Evidence: 01, 02, 03, 04, 05.

### 2. Grade/GPA calculator micro-site (standalone) — 16/20
*Multi-course final-grade calc, multi-board CGPA converter, weighted/edge-case GPA.*
- (a) 3 — strong demand ("most-searched grade question every finals season"); revenue is ads-only, thin.
- (b) 3 — board switcher + multi-course planner differentiate, but calculators are a solved category.
- (c) 5 — pure tools, recurring every finals season.
- (d) 5 — calculators don't rot.
- Evidence: 01 (P24), 03 (keywords #2, #3, #17 TARGET).
- Verdict: best as the flagship *section* of the hub, not a standalone business (too narrow, weak monetization alone).

### 3. Nepal NEB exam-prep platform — 14/20
*Topic-tagged NEB past-paper bank, written step-by-step solutions, NEB worksheet generator; SEE/+2/IOE/Lok Sewa.*
- (a) 4 — demand is massive and observed (Gurukul Nepal 656k-view SEE video; 290k-install Lok Sewa app; paid rivals at Rs 950–2,500 prove willingness to pay). Revenue is real but GATED: AdSense day one; eSewa/Khalti merchant both require business registration + PAN/VAT + tax clearance (verified official) — no $0 route to accepting payments before paperwork. Single-country ad ceiling; brutal seasonality (dead Jun–Dec).
- (b) 4 — topic-tagged NEB bank exists nowhere (paperguides.org proves the format but covers zero NEB); written solutions have no clean owner.
- (c) 4 — the strongest wedge is itself tool-shaped (question/worksheet generator, pure software, no licensing risk).
- (d) 2 — yearly content treadmill (new papers each cycle; a solved paper ≈ 2–4 hrs expert work); NEB paper copyright unverified (ICAN explicitly off-limits); exam-season crunch while the founder is himself in exams.
- Evidence: 06.
- Verdict: strongest *pivot* candidate evaluated; loses to #1 on the brief's own priorities (gated revenue, seasonality, maintenance). Its best insights (NEB/South-Asia-first wedge) are absorbed into #1's differentiation; full NEB bank reserved as Phase 6+ expansion once revenue funds registration.

### 4. Templates/printables + digital products engine — 14/20
*Free template generators as lead magnets → Paddle digital products ($3–$15 band).*
- (a) 4 — Etsy proves willingness to pay ($7–$20 observed); Paddle verified Nepal-eligible; newsletter-bait loop.
- (b) 3 — "free + no email wall" is the wedge, but templates are copyable; generators add defensibility.
- (c) 3 — generators are tools; static PDFs are not.
- (d) 4 — low maintenance once built.
- Evidence: 02 (Etsy/Notion), 03 (#18 TARGET), 04 (price bands).
- Verdict: ideal *pillar and monetization engine inside* the hub, weak as a standalone (no traffic engine of its own).

### 5. Exam-oriented subject study guides (standalone) — 12/20
*Worked-example STEM/engineering guides, formula guides, past-paper frequency analyses.*
- (a) 4 — real demand (P14/P16); affiliates fit (Bookshop.org 10%, Coursera 15–45%); cheat-sheet packs are natural products.
- (b) 4 — exam-oriented, worked-example style is underserved (SparkNotes lit-only; OpenStax textbook-dense).
- (c) 2 — content, not tools; no recurring-visitor engine beyond SEO.
- (d) 2 — highest production cost; guide head terms held by universities/publishers (long-tail grind).
- Evidence: 01, 02, 03 (#9 AVOID head term).
- Verdict: supporting long-tail content (formula guides paired with tools), not the business.

## Evaluated and rejected

### 6. Newsletter-first study business — 10/20
(a) 2 — education sponsorships are the bargain bin ($7.21/1,000 subs, Paved; beehiiv Ad Network payouts to Nepal unverifiable — Stripe Express required). Products possible but list growth is the whole game. (b) 3 (c) 2 (d) 3 — batchable but relentless cadence; cadence death is the #1 killer (Austin Scholar precedent). Verdict: best as an *audience layer on top of tools*, not the business. Evidence: 07.

### 7. Study accountability platform — 8/20
(a) 2 — demand real (Study Together 1M+ Discord members observed) but monetization only at moderated scale. (b) 3 — only wedge is timezone-aware niche matching. (c) 2 (d) 1 — moderation/safety operation (minors, harassment reports), 1–3 hrs/day forever, empty-room cold start. Fails minimal-maintenance catastrophically. Evidence: 01 (P30), 07.

### 8. Study-skills/productivity blog — 7/20
(a) 2 — biggest raw demand (procrastination #1 theme) but creator-dominated (Ali Abdaal 8M+ followers) and advice monetizes worst. (b) 2 (c) 1 (d) 2. Evidence: 01, 02, 05.

### 9. Creator-led exam-prep brand (YouTube/shorts) — 6/20
(a) 2 — demand proven (Lofi Girl 15.8M subs observed; Nepal YPP-eligible) but ~$0.50–$2 CPMs on student audiences; months of 4–10 hrs/video for triple-digit views typical. (b) 2 (c) 1 (d) 1 — 8–12+ hrs/week treadmill competing directly with the founder's studies. Worst maintenance fit. Evidence: 07.

---

## Bottom line

The original idea survived the challenge — not from attachment, but because it scores 20/20 on the brief's own priorities and the strongest pivot candidate (Nepal NEB exam-prep, 14/20) loses on gated revenue, seasonality, and maintenance while its best insights fold into the winner as differentiation. Final top 5 for the business plan: **toolkit hub, grade calculators, Nepal NEB exam-prep, templates/products engine, subject guides** — with the newsletter as a recommended audience layer and the rest rejected with reasons.
