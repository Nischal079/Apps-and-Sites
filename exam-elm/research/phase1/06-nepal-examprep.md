### 1.4 Indian overlap (CBSE/JEE/NEET)
Minimal for NEB-specific tools — Nepal runs its own board, syllabus, and grading (see §3.4). Shared-tool opportunity exists only at the **generic layer** (formula sheets, calculators, MCQ practice engines), not content. One app (Smartboard, Pankaj Universal AI) explicitly targets "NEET, JEE, CBSE or CA" alongside Nepal — i.e., it treats them as separate content tracks. **Verdict: don't build for India; build NEB-native, keep the tooling generic enough to port.**

### 2.3 Adjacent (not direct competitors — potential collaborators)

**paperguides.org** — https://paperguides.org/ (opened and read directly, 2026-11-04). Nepal-built, open-source, free-forever. Offers: past papers, syllabus-mapped student notes, a **question generator** (custom worksheets by topic/difficulty/component, print-ready PDF), and a **per-topic question bank** ("every question, sorted by what it tests", with mark schemes). **Coverage is A Levels (CAIE) + Kathmandu University only — zero NEB SEE/+2 coverage.** That is the single most important finding about this site: its *format* (topic-tagged bank + generator) is exactly the gap in the NEB market, and it doesn't serve NEB at all. ## 3. GAPS (ranked by evidence)

1. **Topic-tagged NEB past-paper bank.** Every NEB paper archive is organized by *year/province* (blog posts), never by *topic*. paperguides.org proves the UX works (generator + per-topic index) but only for A Levels/KU. A "practice by topic, not by paper" bank for SEE/+2 Physics/Chemistry/Maths/Biology would be genuinely new. Feasibility caveat: requires transcribing/tagging thousands of questions — the highest-effort build here.
2. **Written step-by-step solutions to NEB papers.** Solutions exist as scattered YouTube videos (high friction: can't skim, can't print) and thin blog PDFs. A clean, text-based, searchable, printable solved-paper library for SEE/+2 core subjects has no clear owner. Sajha Notes (25 solved papers) and Bidhya Mandir are closest but incomplete and science-skewed.
3. **A question generator / custom worksheet tool for NEB.** "Give me 12 mixed questions on Organic Chemistry, medium difficulty, printable" — exists for A Levels (paperguides.org), nothing equivalent for NEB. Pure software; no content licensing needed if questions are original/model-set style.
4. **Mobile-first, offline-capable free tools.** Most Play Store NEB apps are ad-heavy, stale, or removed (e.g., SEE Estudy, 4.5k installs, removed Jan 2026). A fast PWA (past papers + GPA calc + formula sheets, offline) would beat the app graveyard at $0 distribution cost.
5. **NEB GPA/percentage calculator with the real credit-hour math.** Partially filled: bikrambhujel.com.np has a solid calculator following the Letter Grading Directive 2078 (theory/internal graded separately, credit-hour weighted); gpatopercentage.thenepal.io and Padandas cover conversions. Gap is narrow: a *subject-wise "what do I need in the final to get an A"* planner, or a calculator embedded inside a bigger prep hub, not a standalone page.
6. **English-medium quality explanations.** Most free explanation content is Nepali-medium video; written English-medium NEB explanations (for English-medium schools and students aiming abroad) are thin.
7. **Post-SEE bridge content.** studynotesnepal.com's "top-10 college entrance model questions" is a real micro-niche (St. Xavier's, Budhanilkantha, etc. entrances) that is old and unmaintained — low competition, small but motivated audience.

## 4. MONETIZATION — DOMESTIC ANGLE (verified from official pages)

### 4.1 eSewa merchant — VERIFIED (official blog, esewa.com.np)
- **Requirements** (https://blog.esewa.com.np/interested-in-becoming-an-esewa-merchant-heres-how-you-can-do-it/): PAN/VAT certificate, business registration certificate, owner's citizenship, 2 PP photos, recent tax clearance certificate, bank account at a member bank, website.
- **Business QR request** (https://blog.esewa.com.np/qr-request/): needs a registered business with PAN; requests rejected without proof of business.
- **Fees:** "Domestic payments through eSewa QR remain completely free of charge" (https://blog.esewa.com.np/alipay-on-esewa-business-qr — FAQ on cross-border; 1.95% applies only to international Alipay+/UPI receipts).
- **Verdict for a solo student founder:** not available on day one. Needs at minimum a registered sole proprietorship + PAN + tax clearance. Achievable later, but it is a paperwork project, not a signup form.

### 4.2 Khalti merchant / payment gateway — VERIFIED (official, khalti.com)
- **Requirements** (https://khalti.com/payment-gateway/): sign up at khalti.com/merchant, fill business KYC; required docs: **Company Registration Certificate, PAN/VAT certificate, Latest Tax Clearance, logo**; "Merchant has to be registered at any official institutions, recognized by the government of Nepal… Yes, the company must be registered in Nepal."
- **Fees** (https://khalti.com/info/terms/merchant/): "one time API Fee and Annual Account Maintenance Charge applicable from the 2nd year"; per-transaction service charge on percentage basis. Exact rates via merchant@khalti.com (not published on page).
- **Sandbox:** test keys free; "Even after successful integration, you won't be able to receive payments above NPR 200 per transaction. Fill the KYC form and contact us… to remove the limits" (https://docs.khalti.com/getting-started/).
- **Verdict:** same story as eSewa — real merchant acceptance needs a registered entity + tax docs. Khalti Bazaar (no-website selling) also requires company registration. Not day-one viable for an unregistered student.

### 4.3 IME Pay — checked, WEAK standalone option
- IME Pay is now published by **IME Khalti Limited** (https://www.appbrain.com/app/ime-pay-mobile-digital-wallet/com.swifttechnology.imepay) — the wallet is being folded into the Khalti ecosystem (also noted by https://paymentgatewaynepal.com/). Merchant/agent onboarding needs PAN + shop registration. No advantage over eSewa/Khalti for a new founder; treat as a future add-on, not a primary rail.

### 4.4 Google AdSense — VERIFIED (official Google support pages)
- **Nepal is on the official AdSense availability list** (https://support.google.com/adsense/answer/13402307?hl=en — APAC table includes Nepal).
- **Payout methods for Nepal:** Check + **Wire transfer** (https://support.google.com/admob/answer/1714397?hl=en — APAC row: "Nepal | Yes | No | Yes | No" for Check/EFT/Wire/Hyperwallet).
- Caveats: wire needs a SWIFT-capable bank account; $100 threshold; Nepal has no US tax treaty, so US withholding can apply without proper W-8BEN handling (reported by third-party source https://www.lilachbullock.com/youtube-monetization-nepal-uae/ — treat CPM/withholding specifics as that author's reporting, not Google's). Nepali-audience CPMs are low (same source estimates ~$0.50–$2; **unverified, do not use in projections**).
- **Verdict:** AdSense is the only day-one, $0, no-paperwork monetization available. Revenue will be small until traffic is large — plan for it as the Phase-1 monetization, not wallet payments.

### 4.5 Affiliate — VERIFIED via Daraz's own T&Cs as reported
- **Daraz Affiliate Program Nepal exists** (https://www.gadgetbytenepal.com/daraz-affiliate-program/; T&C details via https://makemoneyonline.lk/daraz-affiliate-program/ citing Daraz Nepal T&Cs): free signup, commissions up to ~10% by category, **NPR 1,500 minimum payout**, monthly payout in NPR, 7-day cookie, rates revisable at Daraz's discretion. Requires ability to submit VAT/PAN invoice — mild friction for a student.
- Fit: only relevant if the site recommends physical products (guidebooks, stationery, phones for study). Secondary at best for an exam-prep content site.
- International affiliates (Amazon etc.) pay via wire/Payoneer — not Nepal-friendly for small sums; deprioritized.

### 4.6 Domestic-payment verdict (for the parent report)
**Day one: AdSense only.** eSewa and Khalti merchant acceptance both require a registered business + PAN/VAT + tax clearance — a paperwork project for later, not a signup. Domestic QR receipt is free of charge once set up (eSewa), and Khalti charges a one-time API fee + per-transaction %. If/when the founder registers a sole proprietorship (possible at a ward office with citizenship + PAN), eSewa merchant becomes the first wallet rail to add; Khalti second. Daraz affiliate is a real but marginal add-on. No credible $0 route to accepting student payments exists before business registration.

## 5. COSTS — honest $0 accounting

### 5.1 Domain — VERIFIED free
- **.np domains are free** via the official registry portal **register.com.np**, run by Mercantile Communication Pvt. Ltd. Personal .com.np: domain must match the applicant's name on citizenship/passport/driving license/voter card; cover letter + ID scan (JPG ≤200KB); approval typically 1–4 working days. Company .com.np: registration certificate + PAN. (https://hamrosikshya.com/blog/free-com-np-domain-nepal, http://www.bikrambhujel.com.np/2025/01/step-by-step-guide-to-registering-free.html, https://www.vivekkarn.com.np/how-to-make-a-website-in-nepal-for-free/)
- Caveat: a *brand* name domain (not your personal name) as an individual is harder — the name-match rule pushes individuals toward personal-name domains; a business-name domain wants business docs. Workaround used by Nepali student devs: personal-name .com.np + branded site title, or just use a free subdomain. Either way, **$0 is real**.

### 5.2 Hosting / stack — $0 is real
- Static site: **GitHub Pages / Netlify / Cloudflare Pages** — free, custom .np domain supported (register.com.np needs nameservers; Cloudflare free DNS works — per https://www.vivekkarn.com.np/how-to-make-a-website-in-nepal-for-free/).
- Dynamic bits (question bank search, generator): can stay static (JSON + client-side JS) at this scale. Database/auth only needed if adding user accounts — defer.
- PDFs: host on the same static host or archive.org; bandwidth is the only scaling risk, and it's far off.

### 5.3 Maintenance burden (all labeled ESTIMATES — judgment, not measured data)

**(a) Past-paper bank site**
- Per exam cycle: collect that season's papers (SEE ~7 provinces × ~8 subjects + GI papers; +2 similar), convert/organize, fix broken links, post routine/result updates.
- **Estimate: 6–10 hrs/week during exam season (Feb–May), 1–2 hrs/week off-season.** Mostly mechanical; could be reduced with a submission form + community uploads.

**(b) NEB-focused tools (grade/GPA calculators, formula sheets, countdowns)**
- Build once (~20–40 hrs for a polished calculator + formula-sheet section). Then near-zero: update only when NEB amends the grading directive (last noted amendment: 2081 per https://www.bikrambhujel.com.np/p/neb-gpa-calculator.html).
- **Estimate: 0–1 hr/week steady state.**

**(c) Guides / solved-questions site**
- The expensive one. A proper step-by-step solution for one full paper ≈ 2–4 hrs of expert work; covering 5 years × 4 core subjects ≈ hundreds of hours. Quality bar matters (wrong solutions destroy trust).
- **Estimate: 8–15 hrs/week to build a meaningful library in 90 days; 3–5 hrs/week to maintain/extend.** Realistic only with (i) narrow subject focus (e.g., Class 12 Physics + Chemistry only), or (ii) community contributions with review, or (iii) AI-assisted drafting with human verification (accuracy risk must be managed).

## 6. RISKS

1. **Copyright on past papers — MIXED, be careful.**
   - **CA Nepal (ICAN): explicit prohibition.** ICAN's suggested-answers PDFs state: "The exam questions and solutions… cannot be published in any form (paper or soft copy), or sold for profit in any way, without first gaining the express permission of ICAN." (https://en.ican.org.np/_browsable/file/downloads/CA_Membership_Exams_Suggested_Answer_March_2026.pdf) → **Do not build on CA papers without permission.**
   - **NEB papers: could not verify.** No official NEB copyright statement found. Precedent cuts both ways: dozens of Nepali sites host NEB papers openly with no visible enforcement, but PhysicsNP's terms treat NEB/CBSE papers as "intellectual property of their respective owners/boards" and offer rights-holder takedown (https://www.physicsnp.com/terms-and-conditions.html). **Honest position: assume NEB owns its papers; risk of a takedown notice is low but nonzero. Mitigation: transcribe/paraphrase into topic-tagged *original* practice questions (like paperguides.org's generator model) rather than redistributing board PDFs wholesale, and honor any takedown immediately.**
2. **Extreme seasonality.** Demand concentrates around: SEE (exams Chaitra/Mar–Apr, prep peak Jan–Mar), Class 12 (exams Baisakh/Apr–May, prep peak Feb–Apr), results (May–Jul), grade-increment exams (Shrawan/Jun–Jul). Jun–Dec is a trough. A content business must either bank content in the trough or add a year-round track (Lok Sewa/IOE) to smooth it.
3. **Free competition is the default.** YouTube (Gurukul Nepal's 656k-view SEE videos), Facebook/TikTok solution drops, free apps (290k-install Loksewa Tayari), and a dozen SEO blogs mean "free past papers" is not a differentiator — *organization, solutions quality, and tools* are.
4. **Single-country ceiling.** Nepal's student population and Nepali-audience ad rates are small (third-party CPM estimates ~$0.50–$2, unverified). This is a "thousands of dollars per year at scale" business, not a venture-scale one — which is fine for the mission's "sustainable online income" goal but must be sized honestly.
5. **Platform risk on distribution.** Facebook/YouTube/TikTok algorithm changes can throttle the main free acquisition channels; the owned site + email/WhatsApp list is the hedge.
6. **Content-accuracy risk.** Wrong solutions or outdated grading info (NEB amended its directive in 2081) kill trust fast in an exam product. Needs a correction workflow (Sajha Notes' per-note report button is the model to copy).
7. **Founder bandwidth.** The solved-solutions track is the most differentiated but also the most time-intensive; a student founder in exam cycles of his own must scope to 1–2 subjects or it will stall.

## 7. BOTTOM LINE FOR THE PIVOT DECISION

- **Demand is real and observable** (hundreds of thousands of views on NEB solution videos; 290k installs on a free Lok Sewa app; paid competitors charging Rs 199–2,500 proving willingness to pay in adjacent tracks).
- **The raw-paper archive is commoditized** (a dozen free blogs). The **open gaps are structure and tools**: topic-tagged question banks, a worksheet generator, quality written solutions, offline-capable mobile tools — with paperguides.org as the proven format reference that *doesn't cover NEB*.
- **$0 startup is genuinely feasible** (free .np domain + free static hosting). **$0 monetization on day one = AdSense only**; wallet payments (eSewa/Khalti) require business registration + PAN + tax clearance — a later step, not a launch blocker.
- **Biggest risks:** NEB paper copyright is unverified (mitigate via original/transcribed practice content + takedown readiness); ICAN papers are explicitly off-limits; demand is brutally seasonal.

*Suggested narrowest viable wedge (opinion, for parent synthesis): a free, fast, mobile-friendly "NEB Question Bank by Topic + worksheet generator" for 2–3 Class 12 science subjects, on a free .com.np domain, AdSense from day one, YouTube/TikTok solution clips as acquisition — expanding to solved papers and Lok Sewa/IOE tracks only after traction.*
