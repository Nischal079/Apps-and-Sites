# Phase 1 — Monetization Realism (Nepal-based owner, $0 budget)

**Date:** 2026-11-04
**Project:** Student Success Hub (free educational resource platform; owner is a student in Nepal; audience global)
**Workstream:** KEYWORD DEMAND + MONETIZATION — Part B

**Core constraint, verified:** As of Sept–Oct 2026, **Stripe does not support Nepal-based accounts** and **PayPal accounts registered in Nepal can send but cannot receive/withdraw funds** (nepinsights.com, Sept 2026, citing Nepal Rastra Bank policy status). Any platform whose only payout rails are Stripe or PayPal is therefore **unusable** for a Nepal-resident creator without a foreign account. Every verdict below was checked against official help/docs pages or Nepal-specific reporting — not assumed.

---

## A. Display advertising

### 1. Google AdSense — ✅ VERIFIED AVAILABLE in Nepal
- **Source:** Google's official payment-methods help table lists **Nepal: Check + Wire Transfer** for website AdSense (no EFT, no PayPal Hyperwallet). (support.google.com/admob/answer/1714397 — the AdSense/AdMob payments help article; also summarized at lilachbullock.com, Oct 2026)
- **Requirements:** 18+; original, policy-compliant content; site ownership verified; address verification (PIN); tax information (non-US publishers complete a W-8BEN); no official minimum traffic — approval is a manual content/policy review.
- **Payouts:** $100 payment threshold; monthly cycle (paid 21st–26th for prior month once threshold met); wire transfer or check for Nepal.
- **Realistic revenue timing:** AdSense can be applied for from day one of a content-complete site, but meaningful revenue only follows meaningful traffic. Nepal-sourced traffic earns far lower RPMs than US/UK/CA/AU traffic — traffic geography is the single biggest revenue lever (theworldofonlineearnings.com, Aug 2026). **Expect $0–single digits/month for the first 3–6 months.**
- **Verdict: primary ad option; apply once the site has 20–30 quality posts + required pages (Privacy, About, Contact).**

### 2. Journey by Mediavine — ⚠️ AVAILABLE but threshold is the blocker
- **Source:** 2026 requirements (outrank.so / soloopslab.com, checked against Mediavine's own pages Oct 2026): **1,000 sessions from tier-1 countries (US, Canada, UK, Australia) within 30 days**, Grow plugin running 30+ days, ~4 months domain history; 70% revenue share, Net 65.
- **Nepal relevance:** No country ban on Nepal publishers, but if the audience skews Nepali/South-Asian, the tier-1 session bar is hard to clear. Only realistic if the site wins US/UK search traffic.
- **Verdict: long-term upgrade path (12–24 months), not a Phase-1 play.**

### 3. Mediavine (full) / Raptive — ❌ NOT realistic in 90 days
- Full Mediavine now requires **$5,000+ annual ad revenue** (replaced the old 50k-session rule); Raptive requires 25,000 monthly pageviews with tier-1 majority. Both are year-2+ targets. (outrank.so, Oct 2026)

## B. Affiliate marketing

### 4. Amazon Associates — ✅ CAN JOIN from Nepal · ⚠️ PAYOUT IS THE FRICTION
- **Eligibility:** Amazon accepts affiliates from any country, including Nepal (themeisle.com; Nepal-specific walkthroughs at bipinbudhathoki.com.np, daphesansar.com.np). No Amazon presence in Nepal is required — join the amazon.com program.
- **Requirements:** A qualifying site with original content (~10+ unique posts is the commonly cited rule of thumb); account review; **3 qualifying sales within 180 days** to stay in the program (affiliatemarketingokulu.com, Oct 2026).
- **Payouts (the catch):** Direct bank deposit covers ~52 countries (US/UK/Eurozone currencies) — **Nepal is not covered**. Nepali affiliates realistically get paid via **Amazon gift cards**, **check**, or a **Payoneer US virtual bank account** (widely used workaround per Nepal affiliate guides). Non-US persons complete a W-8BEN; US tax withholding may apply.
- **Relevance:** Low for a pure study-resource site unless it recommends study gear/books. Commission rates vary by category — check Amazon's current Commission Income Statement; rates have been cut before (2020), so don't build the business on this.
- **Verdict: joinable and usable with a Payoneer workaround; minor/secondary revenue at best for this niche.**

### 5. Daraz Affiliate (Nepal) — ✅ VERIFIED AVAILABLE in Nepal
- **Source:** Daraz runs its own affiliate portal per country, including **daraz.com.np** (way2earning.com 2026 update; lilachbullock.com, Oct 2026; ideanepalhub.com 2026).
- **Requirements:** Website/YouTube/active social channel (blank accounts rejected); Nepal affiliates submit VAT/PAN invoice.
- **Terms:** Commissions ~1–11%+ by category (electronics 1–3%, fashion/beauty up to 8–12%+); cookie window commonly ~7 days; payouts held until return window closes (first payment can take 10–12 weeks); **minimum withdrawal NPR 1,500/month, paid in NPR** via bank transfer (way2earning.com); payouts also reported via eSewa/bank (gurukulzone.com).
- **Relevance:** Only for product recommendations (stationery, gadgets, books) and only monetizes the **Nepali** slice of traffic. Low ceiling for a study-content site.
- **Verdict: available and genuinely local; worth joining once product-recommendation content exists, but expect small returns.**

### 6. Global affiliate networks (CJ Affiliate, Impact, ShareASale, Awin) — ✅ USABLE from Nepal via Payoneer
- **Source:** Nepal affiliate-marketing guides list these as joinable by Nepali marketers with payouts routed through **Payoneer or Wise** (Quora Nepal answer; daphesansar.com.np 2026 roadmap).
- **Relevance:** Highest-value for students: web hosting (Hostinger, Namecheap — commonly cited in Nepal guides), productivity software, online course platforms. These pay $20–$100+ per referral vs. single-digit percentages on Daraz/Amazon.
- **Verdict: best affiliate ROI for the niche; requires Payoneer account + KYC set up early (free to open).**

## C. Digital products (templates, planners, ebooks, worksheets)

### 7. Gumroad — ❌ EFFECTIVELY BLOCKED for Nepal creators
- **Source:** GetPaidAtlas's country-by-country payout audit (checked 2026-10-30, citing Gumroad's own "Getting paid" help page): **Nepal is in the 85-country "PayPal only (Unclear)" bucket** — Gumroad pays Nepal creators only via PayPal (2% payout fee), and **PayPal accounts in Nepal cannot receive money**. Gumroad explicitly does not support Payoneer, Wise, wire transfer, or check. Minimum payout $100; fees 10% + $0.50 per direct sale (+2.9% + $0.30 processing).
- **Verdict: unusable without a foreign-registered PayPal/bank account. Do not build on Gumroad.**

### 8. Payhip — ❌ BLOCKED natively in Nepal
- **Source:** Payhip does not process cards itself — sellers must connect their **own Stripe or PayPal** account (inkfluenceai.com review, checked against Payhip help center Oct 2026). Neither pays out to Nepal. A Nepali blogger documented the only workaround: a Stripe account opened via a trusted contact abroad (nepbloggers.com).
- **Terms (for reference):** Free plan = 5% per sale; Plus $29/mo = 2%; Pro $99/mo = 0%. Handles EU/UK VAT and US/CA sales tax.
- **Verdict: blocked without a foreign payment account. Revisit only if payout situation changes.**

### 9. Ko-fi — ❌ BLOCKED in Nepal
- **Source:** Ko-fi pays creators **directly into their own PayPal or Stripe account** (help.ko-fi.com: "What payment methods are available on Ko-fi"). Neither supports receiving in Nepal. Third-party comparisons confirm payouts limited to ~35–40 Stripe/PayPal-supported countries (spondula.com, picktheplatform.com).
- **Verdict: unusable. Same story for Patreon (Stripe/PayPal-based payouts).**

### 10. Buy Me a Coffee — ❌ BLOCKED in Nepal
- **Source:** Official help article "Supported Countries for Payouts on Buy Me a Coffee" (updated 2026-10-09): payouts **only in Stripe-supported countries** (44 Standard Connect + 5 Express). **Nepal is not listed.** 5% platform fee + processing; $10 minimum; weekly Wednesday payouts.
- **Verdict: unusable. (A Nepal-built alternative, "Buy Me a Chiya" — buymeachiya.vijaykarn.com.np — exists as a DIY tipping page using the creator's own eSewa/Khalti/bank details with zero middleman; worth noting for donation UX ideas.)**

### 11. Lemon Squeezy — ❌ BLOCKED for Nepal payouts
- **Source:** GetPaidAtlas seller-country comparison (checked 2026-10-30 against Lemon Squeezy's official supported-countries docs): **Nepal = "Unclear" (PayPal-only, and PayPal can't receive in Nepal)** — i.e., no bank payout route. Additionally, Lemon Squeezy (Stripe-owned since 2024) is migrating sellers to **Stripe Managed Payments, whose eligible countries exclude Nepal** (US, CA, UK, EU, CH, NO, AU, HK, JP, SG only). Fees 5% + $0.50 (+1.5% international).
- **Verdict: unusable for a Nepal-based seller.**

### 12. Paddle — ✅ VERIFIED VIABLE — best digital-product route
- **Source:** A 2026 seller-country comparison checked Paddle's official unsupported-countries list on 2026-10-18: **Nepal is NOT on Paddle's blocklist** ("every business still goes through Paddle's review") — zealden.com. Real-world proof: **Nepali builders already sell through Paddle** — Sugam Pokharel's Toolorah (sugamdev.com: "Stripe does not operate in Nepal… Toolorah runs on Paddle instead, as merchant of record") and **Programiz PRO, operated from Nepal**, lists Paddle as a payment processor in its Terms of Service (programiz.pro).
- **Terms:** Merchant of record (handles global VAT/sales tax, fraud, chargebacks); **5% + $0.50 per transaction**; payouts via **bank wire or Payoneer** (G2 review from a Nepal-based digital-download seller confirms Payoneer payouts work); stricter approval review (sellers report rejections without reasons); products under ~$10 may face custom terms (editorialge.com).
- **Requirements/identity:** Business verification during Paddle's review; creator still owes **Nepal income tax** on payouts (Paddle handles indirect taxes only). Foreign earnings are export proceeds under NRB rules — expect bank-level documentation and possible tax deduction at source on remittance (nepinsights.com).
- **Realistic revenue timing:** Viable once there's an audience and a product (premium planner packs, scholarship letter templates, printable bundles). Not a day-one earner.
- **Verdict: THE digital-product platform for this project. Set up when the first paid product is ready, not before.**

### 13. Freemius — ✅ VERIFIED AVAILABLE (but niche-fit is poor)
- **Source:** Freemius's official "Supported Countries" doc: works with software makers in **190+ countries**, payouts via **PayPal MassPay, Payoneer, wire (SWIFT/IBAN), or Wise**; $100 minimum; explicitly answers "Can I still use Freemius if Stripe/PayPal is not supported in my country? — **Yes**." (freemius.com/pricing, freemius.com/help/documentation/selling-with-freemius/supported-countries)
- **Fit:** Freemius is built for **WordPress plugins/themes and software**, not PDF templates/ebooks. Only relevant if the Hub later ships interactive tools as plugins/apps.
- **Verdict: available, but wrong product fit for Phase 1–2. Keep as a fallback for software products.**

## D. Donations / tips

### 14. Buy Me Momo (Nepal-native) — ✅ VERIFIED AVAILABLE
- **Source:** techpana.com (Dec 2025): Nepali tipping platform built because "Buy Me a Coffee or Patreon cannot be fully used in Nepal… You need Stripe or PayPal to receive payments, which are not available here." Payments via **Connect IPS, eSewa, Khalti** (and Stripe for international supporters); **7% platform fee**, taxes deducted, payouts to creator's Nepali bank account **every 15 days**; Rs 350,000+ paid to creators in its first 100 days.
- **Verdict: the only turnkey donation rail that actually works for a Nepal-based creator. Join from day one — costs nothing, monetizes goodwill.**

### 15. Direct eSewa / Khalti / bank transfer links — ✅ AVAILABLE
- A simple "support us" page with the owner's eSewa/Khalti ID or QR (the "Buy Me a Chiya" DIY model) has zero fees and zero approval. Works for Nepali supporters; international supporters need other rails.
- **Verdict: free to set up; include alongside Buy Me Momo.**

## Realistic revenue-timing note (first 90 days)

**Months 0–3: expect ~$0.** The honest sequence is: (1) set up Buy Me Momo + direct wallet links on day one (zero cost, captures any early goodwill); (2) apply for AdSense once 20–30 quality posts exist (approval is the milestone, not revenue); (3) join Daraz Affiliate + one global network (Impact/CJ) via a Payoneer account (free to open; do KYC early since verification takes time); (4) build the email list and traffic toward the **Paddle-powered first digital product** (premium template pack) in months 3–6. No meaningful income before month 4–6 under organic growth; the 90-day goal is *monetization infrastructure + traffic*, not revenue.

**Tax/admin note:** Affiliate and ad income is taxable in Nepal as self-employed/business income and must be declared to the IRD; foreign earnings arrive as export proceeds via formal banking channels with possible withholding at source (daphesansar.com.np; nepinsights.com). Paddle handles VAT/sales tax as merchant of record — Nepal income tax remains the creator's responsibility.

## Limitations
- Platform policies change: payout-country lists, fees, and thresholds were checked 2026-10-18 – 2026-11-04 via official pages and reputable third-party audits; **re-verify at signup**.
- Third-party country audits (GetPaidAtlas, zealden.com) are secondary sources — used only where they cite official docs, and official pages are linked as primary sources.
- No live account testing was performed (no signups, no purchases); approval strictness (e.g., Paddle's review, AdSense's content review) is reported, not experienced.
- PayPal/Stripe Nepal status may evolve — Nepal Rastra Bank was developing an international-gateway policy as of Sept 2026 (nepinsights.com); if PayPal receiving opens in Nepal, Gumroad/Ko-fi/Buy Me a Coffee verdicts flip.
- Revenue estimates are deliberately qualitative; actual RPMs, conversion rates, and timelines depend on traffic volume and geography, which are unknown pre-launch.
