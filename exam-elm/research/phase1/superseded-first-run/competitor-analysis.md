## Per-site breakdown

### 1. Khan Academy (khanacademy.org)
- **Offer:** Free video lessons, articles, practice exercises across ~840 courses (Pre-K to early college, SAT prep, NCERT/India content), plus Khanmigo AI tutor; personalized dashboards.
- **Business model:** 501(c)(3) nonprofit. Record revenue of **$117.5M** in the year to June 2025 (vs $59.3M in 2021), almost entirely donations: $90.4M contributions/grants vs $22.2M program services; Khanmigo district licenses are an emerging B2B line. Source: [ClassCentral tax-returns analysis](https://www.classcentral.com/report/khan-academy-tax-returns-analysis/)
- **Traffic signal:** 185M+ registered learners; ~8.5M monthly active across 190+ countries; ~105M yearly active learners (SY24-25, company-reported via ClassCentral). [Same source](https://www.classcentral.com/report/khan-academy-tax-returns-analysis/)
- **Strength:** Free forever, no ads/paywalls; deep trust with schools and parents; efficacy studies (20–30% learning gains at 18+ hours engagement, per SY24-25 report via CheckThat).
- **Gap:** Heavy K-12 curriculum focus; very little on *how to study* (methods, productivity, exam strategy); institutional/donor funding model means slow iteration on tools students actually want.

### 2. Quizlet (quizlet.com)
- **Offer:** 700M+ user-generated flashcard sets; Learn, Test, Match modes; AI tools (Q-Chat, Magic Notes, note-to-flashcard).
- **Business model:** Freemium. Quizlet Plus $35.99/yr web ($44.99/yr iOS — 25–40% iOS premium); paid tier limits: ~3 practice tests/mo, 3 Q&A solutions/mo, 20 Learn rounds/mo on Plus; advertising on free tier. Sources: [rapidevelopers.com/clone/quizlet](https://www.rapidevelopers.com/clone/quizlet), [learnclash.com/blog/kahoot-vs-quizlet](https://learnclash.com/blog/kahoot-vs-quizlet)
- **Traffic signal:** Claims "two out of every three high school students"; estimates range 50M–70M monthly active users across sources (company claims 60M+ MAU; learnclash reports ~50M 2024–25). Valued at $1B (Series C, 2020). Revenue ~$139M/yr is third-party estimate — **not publicly verifiable**.
- **Strength:** Largest flashcard library on earth; teacher network (3M+ teachers) drives classroom adoption; mobile-first.
- **Gap:** Progressive paywalling of previously free features fuels resentment ("a shell of itself", per Trustpilot reviewers quoted at rapidevelopers); flashcards-only — no study planning, productivity systems, or curated study-method content.

### 3. Studocu (studocu.com)
- **Offer:** Peer-to-peer document sharing: lecture notes, summaries, past exams organized by university and course; Study AI assistant (summaries, quizzes, flashcards from uploads).
- **Business model:** Freemium — ~80% open access with ads; Premium from ~$5/mo annual to ~$15/mo monthly; upload-to-unlock (3–5 docs per upload). Sources: [brightseotools.com Studocu review](https://brightseotools.com/post/StuDocu-Review-&-Top-Alternatives), [canvasbusinessmodel.com](https://businessmodelcanvastemplate.com/blogs/growth-strategy/studocu-growth-strategy)
- **Traffic signal:** 30M+ registered users, 15M+ documents (2025, per brightseotools); 70M users by early 2025 claimed elsewhere; mobile app passed 50M downloads (2025); ARR figures (€110M+ for 2025) appear on AI-generated business-model profile sites — **treat as approximate, not verified**.
- **Strength:** Best hyper-local, course-specific coverage (2,500+ university sub-portals); network effects drive uploads; strong in EMEA/LatAm emerging markets.
- **Gap:** Content quality varies wildly (student uploads, no editorial); copyright takedown exposure; the useful stuff sits behind Premium; no structured *learning-to-learn* content.

### 4. Course Hero (coursehero.com)
- **Offer:** Course-specific study documents (notes, past quizzes, study guides); textbook solutions; 24/7 on-demand tutoring; AI study tools; owns Symbolab and LitCharts.
- **Business model:** Freemium "give-to-get" unlocks model. Premier subscription: $39.99/mo monthly, ~$19.95/mo quarterly, ~$9.95/mo annual ($119.40/yr); 30 document unlocks/mo; extra tutor questions $3 each. Sources: [makeheadway.com/blog/course-hero-review/](https://makeheadway.com/blog/course-hero-review/), [myengineeringbuddy.com/blog/course-hero-review-pricing-alternatives-and-faqs/](https://www.myengineeringbuddy.com/blog/course-hero-review-pricing-alternatives-and-faqs/)
- **Traffic signal:** 60M+ registered users claimed (via [sharetribe.com](https://www.sharetribe.com/create/how-to-build-website-like-coursehero/)); estimated annual revenue $200M+ (unverified); 70M+ study resources claimed (owned by Learneo).
- **Strength:** Unmatched course-specific document coverage (40 subjects); tutoring upsell; acquisitions (Symbolab, LitCharts) broaden the suite.
- **Gap:** $40/mo entry is steep for students; unlock caps frustrate paying users; reputation problem (cheating associations; 2/5 Trustpilot per [vertechacademy.ca](https://vertechacademy.ca)); useful only if someone uploaded for your exact class.

### 5. Chegg (chegg.com)
- **Offer:** Chegg Study (expert Q&A, step-by-step textbook solutions), Writing, Math Solver, language learning; Chegg Skilling (career courses); AI data services for tech companies.
- **Business model:** Subscription-first (~88–89% of revenue historically): Chegg Study ~$15.95/mo, Study Pack $19.95/mo; enterprise skilling and AI training-data services. Sources: [bizmaa.com/chegg/](https://bizmaa.com/chegg/), [dcfanalyst.com](https://dcfanalyst.com/blogs/overview/chgg-overview)
- **Traffic signal:** Revenue fell from $776M (2021) → ~$716M (2023) → ~$616M (2024); subscribers dropped ~31% to ~3.2M by early 2025; market cap collapsed 99%+ from 2021 peak. The company publicly cited ChatGPT as a driver of declining growth. Sources: [bizmaa.com/chegg/](https://bizmaa.com/chegg/), [k-dense.ai CHGG thesis](https://k-dense.ai/examples/session_20260203_085912_030e6e80a419/writing_outputs/final/CHGG_Short_Thesis.pdf)
- **Strength:** Still-strong brand; curated verified solutions; proprietary first-party learning data; Skilling line growing (~$17.6M in Q1 2026, +9% YoY).
- **Gap:** Generative AI (ChatGPT/Claude/Gemini) replicates the core Q&A value for free — the cautionary tale: a paid homework-help moat is not durable; high price for students in developing economies.

### 6. SparkNotes (sparknotes.com)
- **Offer:** Study guides for 1,000+ titles (literature, history, science, math); No Fear Shakespeare modern translations; test prep (SAT, ACT, AP); free mobile app.
- **Business model:** Free + advertising (owned by Barnes & Noble since 2001); SparkNotes Plus ($5.99/mo) adds ad-free + mastery quizzes, flashcards, No Fear translations, note-taking; print study guides/SparkCharts sold at B&N. Sources: [makeheadway.com/blog/sparknotes-subjects-explained/](https://makeheadway.com/blog/sparknotes-subjects-explained/), [Wikipedia via mirror](https://wiki.poast.org/wiki/Sparknotes?lang=en)
- **Traffic signal:** Not publicly verifiable as current. (A 2003 article cited 80M hits/month and 5M registered users — historical only, not current evidence.)
- **Strength:** Trusted household brand; excellent free literature summaries; no sign-up required.
- **Gap:** Humanities-heavy with shallow STEM; ad-supported reading experience; virtually no study-skills/productivity content; the 2022 SparkNotes Plus paywall alienated some of its free ethos.

### 7. Study.com
- **Offer:** 4,500+ courses, 79,000 short animated video lessons; homework help, practice tests; ACE-recommended courses earning real transferable college credit (2,000 colleges); teacher resources.
- **Business model:** Pure subscription, no free tier of note: $29.99 (teacher) to $59.99 (Premium) to $95 (College Saver) to $235/mo (College Saver Pro); B2B teacher editions. Sources: [brighterly.com/blog/how-much-is-study-com/](https://brighterly.com/blog/how-much-is-study-com/), [yespress.io/study-com](https://yespress.io/study-com)
- **Traffic signal:** Company reports 30M+ monthly learners & educators; 470,000+ college credits transferred ($475M tuition saved, company claims). Sources: [yespress.io/study-com](https://yespress.io/study-com)
- **Strength:** The college-credit transfer pipeline is a genuine credential moat; enormous video library.
- **Gap:** No free entry point — pricing starts ~$60/mo for students; confusing billing and cancellation complaints (Trustpilot, Reddit, per brighterly); US-centric model irrelevant to most global students.

### 8. Thomas Frank / College Info Geek (thomasjfrank.com, collegeinfogeek.com)
- **Offer:** Free study-tips/productivity blog + YouTube channel (2.9M subscribers); Notion tutorials on second channel; free Notion Fundamentals course; premium Notion templates.
- **Business model:** Creator business: YouTube ads, sponsorships, Skillshare royalties, affiliate income — but the core engine is product: Ultimate Brain ($139) and Creator's Companion ($64–100) templates. Evergreen funnel: free tutorials → paid finished systems. Sources: [startupfounderstories.com](https://startupfounderstories.com/stories/thomas-frank-creators-companion-1m-a-year), [joshmasters.com](https://joshmasters.com/how-thomas-frank-made-2-5m-selling-notion-templates-in-a-5-market/)
- **Traffic signal:** ~2.9M YouTube subscribers (main channel); collegeinfogeek.com ~400–600K monthly visits (per creatorscience); template business did ~$1M in 2022 (~$100K/mo peak), lifetime ~$2.5M from 30K customers at ~99% margin. Sources: [creatorscience.com/podcast/thomas-frank/](https://creatorscience.com/podcast/thomas-frank/), [joshmasters.com](https://joshmasters.com/how-thomas-frank-made-2-5m-selling-notion-templates-in-a-5-market/)
- **Strength:** Proof that a free content funnel into premium *finished systems* (not courses) is a $5-market-defying business; deep niche trust.
- **Gap:** Everything he sells costs $64–139 — priced out of reach for most global students; Notion-dependent (template breaks without Notion); single-person brand = limited content breadth.

### 9. Ali Abdaal (aliabdaal.com)
- **Offer:** YouTube productivity/study content (5.4M subscribers); newsletter, podcast, book (Feel-Good Productivity); Skillshare classes.
- **Business model:** Creator business: AdSense (~$380K in 2021, third-party), sponsorships ($500K+ 2021), Skillshare royalties ($475.7K from 9M+ minutes watched by 100K students), Part-Time YouTuber Academy ($1,495–$4,995). ~$2M/yr estimated total. Sources: [starterstory.com/ali-abdaal-breakdown](https://www.starterstory.com/ali-abdaal-breakdown), [thinkdmg.com](https://thinkdmg.com/how-ali-abdaal-built-a-multi-million-dollar-brand-with-youtube-content-a-small-business-playbook/), [influencerfee.com/influencer/ali-abdaal/](https://influencerfee.com/influencer/ali-abdaal/)
- **Traffic signal:** 5.4M YouTube subscribers (largest productivity channel per influencerfee); first business year ~£1M revenue (AdSense + sponsors + Skillshare).
- **Strength:** Masterclass in audience-first funnel; high-trust personal brand with evidence-based framing; huge reach in the valuable 22–35 professional/student demographic.
- **Gap:** Content has drifted from study tips toward creator-career content; monetization is high-ticket ($1K+ courses) — zero relevance for broke students; passive content only, no interactive study tools.

### 10. Notion templates community (notion.so/templates + Gumroad/Etsy marketplaces)
- **Offer:** Free Notion app with a curated template gallery; independent creator marketplaces (Prototion 1,000+ templates, Notionery); student dashboards, study planners, second-brain systems.
- **Business model:** Notion's freemium SaaS (paid team plans) — the *templates themselves* are a creator economy: free templates as lead magnets (3–8% free→paid conversion reported), paid templates $7–139; Gumroad takes 10% + $0.50/sale. Sources: [zanfia.com](https://zanfia.com/blog/selling-notion-templates-2026-pricing-distribution-and-recurring-revenue/), [craftific.com](https://craftific.com/guides/how-to-sell-notion-templates/), [professionalstoolkit.com](https://professionalstoolkit.com/articles/notion-sell-templates)
- **Traffic signal:** Notion reached ~100M users by 2024 (per craftific); top student-template creators earn $500–10K/mo (top-heavy); Easlo reported $239K in 2022, north of $500K/yr since.
- **Strength:** Students will *buy pre-built systems* — proof that opinionated defaults beat blank canvases; free-template viral loop (duplicate link) is a $0-CAC funnel.
- **Gap:** Blank-canvas problem: a new student faces choice overload; quality of free student templates is wildly inconsistent; templates depend on Notion's platform and its free-plan limits.

### 11. MyStudyLife (mystudylife.com)
- **Offer:** Cross-platform school planner: class schedule, homework/exam tracking, grades, reminders, home-screen widgets; AI "Scout" planner added recently; 187 countries.
- **Business model:** Freemium with subscriptions: MyStudyLife+ £4.99/mo or £29.99/yr (~$6.99/mo, $39.99/yr in USD); Family Connect tiers. Sources: [apptail.io](https://apptail.io/app/my-study-life-school-planner-oxf/romania), [mystudylife.com](https://mystudylife.com/?p=4074)
- **Traffic signal:** 5.9M Android installs (Google Play, via [appbrain.com](https://www.appbrain.com/app/com.virblue.mystudylife)); company claims "10M+ students, 2M+ organized" across 187 countries. Revenue: not publicly verifiable.
- **Strength:** Student-specific planner (not a generic calendar); strong cross-device sync; long-established brand (on Play since 2013).
- **Gap:** Paywalling basics (country selection, more than 5 tasks) caused rating collapse to 2.6★/56K ratings — classic freemium overreach; organization only, zero study content; users advise "just use Notion for free".

### 12. Forest (forestapp.cc, Seekrtech)

- **Business model:** Shifted Dec 2025 from $3.99 one-time purchase to free + Forest Plus subscription (~$59.99/yr); ads on free tier; real tree planting now gated to Plus users (max 5 trees/account), funded from app revenue. ### 13. r/GetStudying (reddit.com/r/GetStudying)
- **Offer:** Community of motivated learners sharing study tips, methods, resources, accountability; volunteer-moderated; free wikis.
- **Business model:** None of its own — free community on Reddit (Reddit monetizes via ads/Reddit Premium). No products, no premium tier.
- **Traffic signal:** ~3.3M members (2026); hit 3M milestone per mod post; growing ~97–121K members/yr. ### 14. AnkiWeb / Anki (apps.ankiweb.net)
- **Offer:** Open-source spaced-repetition flashcard program (desktop, Android, web free); FSRS scheduler; audio/image/LaTeX cards; large shared-deck ecosystem.
- **Business model:** Free everywhere except official iOS app (AnkiMobile, $24.99 one-time), whose sales fund the whole project — no ads, no subscriptions, no VC. - **Strength:** Best-in-class spaced repetition; offline-first; open source; community add-ons and shared decks.
- **Gap:** Notorious learning curve (card templates, settings, FSRS tuning) drives beginners away; no curated content or onboarding guidance; $24.99 iOS cost surprises students; no built-in study guidance or AI assistance.

### 15. GoConqr (goconqr.com, formerly ExamTime)

- **Business model:** Free-to-start with tight free limits (3 subjects, 20 public resources); premium plans for schools/teams; corporate pivot. Sources: [teachersfirst.org](https://teachersfirst.org/single.cfm?id=16722), [samware.co.uk](http://www.samware.co.uk/examtime/)

- **Gap:** Free tier too limited to retain students; dwarfed by Quizlet/Studocu scale (4M vs 30–60M); corporate/enterprise pivot diluted the student focus; stagnant product visibility.

## Gaps to exploit (top opportunities for a new free global platform)

1. **The free "learning-to-learn" layer is owned by no one.** The biggest players are either curriculum content (Khan Academy), memorization tools (Quizlet, Anki), or document dumps (Studocu, Course Hero). The *study methods* space is served by creator personalities (Thomas Frank, Ali Abdaal) whose products are paywalled ($64–139 templates, $1K+ courses) — leaving a wide-open lane for a free, structured library of evidence-based study techniques, productivity systems, and exam strategies, especially localized for non-US students.
2. **Paid-tool overreach creates free-seeking refugees.** Quizlet paywalled formerly free features, MyStudyLife gated basic planning (2.6★ backlash), Forest gated tree-planting, Coursera ended free audits. Each overreach produces a wave of students actively searching for free alternatives — the exact audience a $0-price platform can capture with "free forever" positioning.
3. **Structure is missing where community is strong.** r/GetStudying has 3.3M members but no curation; Notion has templates but a blank-canvas problem; Studocu has documents but no quality control. A platform that combines community wisdom *with* curated, searchable, quality-controlled resources (study guides + templates + tool walkthroughs) fills the trust gap none of them covers.
4. **Emerging-market students are priced out everywhere.** Premium tiers concentrate in the US/Singapore/EU; $40–60/yr subscriptions are prohibitive in South Asia, Africa, LatAm. A globally free platform with regional/localized content (exam boards, local curricula) has no direct free competitor at scale — Khan Academy is the closest and it's K-12 curriculum-focused.
5. **AI has reset expectations — content libraries alone are no longer a moat.** Chegg's collapse shows Q&A/homework-help is now a commodity. The durable edge is trust + curation + community + free price, i.e., brand and network, not content inventory.

## Limitations

- **Traffic/revenue figures are third-party claims, not audits.** Where the source is the company itself (AppBrain/app-store listings, company bios on review sites, creator-reported revenue), figures are noted as claimed. Khan Academy (Form 990) and Coursera (public filings) are the only audited financial figures in this file.
- **Some Studocu figures conflict across sources** (30M+ registered users per brightseotools vs 70M users by early 2025 per businessmodelcanvastemplate; ARR claims of €110M+ appear on AI-generated business-profile pages). Treated as approximate.
- **Quizlet revenue (~$139M/yr) is a third-party estimate and not publicly verifiable**; company is private.
- **MyStudyLife, Forest (revenue), Anki (user counts), GoConqr (revenue)** have no public financial data; "not publicly verifiable" is stated in each section.
- **Subscriber counts for creator channels** (Thomas Frank, Ali Abdaal) are platform-visible approximations that drift daily.
- **No live browsing was available for this research** (no account-state checks, no paywall verification beyond published pages). Pricing reflects pages indexed as of 2026-11 and may have changed since.
