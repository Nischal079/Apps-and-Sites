## Recurring phrasing patterns (for titles/headings)

- **"How do I / How do you …"** dominates: "How do I stop procrastinating", "How do I study for 12 hours per day?", "How do you take notes…"
- **"I have [exam] in [N] days and …"** urgency framing: "I have my final in 3 days for which i have NOT studied at all", "I need advice / feedback on studying for a test in 3 days"
- **"best … ?"** — "best studying methods?", "Best study method for learning proofs (discrete math)?"
- **Confession framing** — "Anyone else go from class topper to barely passing after getting addicted to their phone?", "From topper to below average we grew up.."
- **"Is it worth …?"** — "Is it worth pulling an all nighter to revise?", "Should I stay up on Tuesday night to cram…?"
- **"I can't …"** — "I can't focus", "Can't afford textbooks and materials"
- **Lowercase, typo-tolerant, emotional** — many top posts are lowercase with typos ("apprecciated", "cream 1 night before exam"); SEO titles should mirror the *words* but can normalize casing.
