## Recommendation

**Primary niche: A — the free exam-planning toolkit hub**, with **B (grade calculators)** folded in as the flagship calculator section and **C (free templates)** as the downloads pillar. D (subject guides) becomes supporting long-tail content produced opportunistically; E (study-skills advice) is avoided as a primary lane — advice only appears *paired with a tool* ("how to use the revision planner" guides).

**Positioning (one line):** *Free exam-prep tools that plan your studying for you — no signup, no install, no paywall. South-Asia-friendly.*

### Why A wins
1. **Strongest evidence convergence:** the #1 underserved need (timetable builder), 6 TARGET keywords, and the weakest competitive field all point here. No other candidate has all three.
2. **Blue-ocean tool:** the syllabus coverage tracker has no web incumbent at all — a genuine moat a solo builder can ship in days.
3. **Compounds:** tools link to each other (countdown → daily hours → timetable → syllabus tracker → session logger), creating an ecosystem competitors' single-purpose tools can't match.
4. **Fits the builder:** Nischal's own exam-prep method (past-paper frequency analysis, structured revision) is the product philosophy — authentic, not manufactured.
5. **Fits the constraints:** $0, static hosting, client-side JS, no backend, no AI costs. Every tool works offline-capable from localStorage.
6. **Monetization path is real:** AdSense from day one (Nepal wire payout verified), affiliates on guide pages, Paddle digital products (planners, cheat-sheet packs) once audience exists — all verified Nepal-compatible.

### Why the others lose (as primary niche)
- **B alone** is too narrow (5–6 calculators, then done) and monetizes weakly — but it's a perfect *section* of the hub, and the final-grade calculator is the fastest traffic win.
- **C alone** is copyable and has weaker search demand — but "free, no email wall" templates are the ideal list-builder and Pinterest fuel *inside* the hub.
- **D alone** is Nischal's authentic strength but a content treadmill against university/publisher authority, with the highest production cost — best as opportunistic long-tail support (e.g. formula guides for tools T13).
- **E alone** has the biggest raw demand and the worst everything else (creator-dominated, undifferentiated, weak monetization) — explicitly rejected as primary.

### Risks & mitigations
- **Risk:** Tool keywords are seasonal (finals spikes). *Mitigation:* evergreen planners + South-Asia board coverage smooth seasonality; templates/guides fill gaps.
- **Risk:** A single-purpose competitor copies one tool. *Mitigation:* the moat is the integrated ecosystem + no-signup UX + South-Asia defaults, not any single tool.
- **Risk:** Paddle application review (gating risk for products). *Mitigation:* monetization doesn't depend on it for 6+ months; AdSense + affiliates carry the early phase.
- **Risk:** Google Trends was rate-limited, so keyword verdicts rest on page-1 composition + qualitative signals. *Mitigation:* verdicts are labeled as estimates; re-validate with Search Console data once the site is live (Phase 4).
