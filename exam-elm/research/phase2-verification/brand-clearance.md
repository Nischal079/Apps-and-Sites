## Verdict: BLOCKED
"StudyPilot" is in active commercial use by multiple education products across NZ, Australia, the UK, Germany, and globally (incl. an exact-name Google Play app and a funded 2026 startup), the .com is taken and parked, and 4 of 5 key social handles are taken. Using it risks user confusion and takedown/impersonation problems. Recommend abandoning the name.
