What it is

What is ?

is a free toolkit of study productivity tools built for students anywhere in the world. Instead of generic planners that assume one country's school system, it ships four focused tools: a study timetable generator, a grade calculator, a syllabus coverage tracker, and an exam countdown planner.

Every tool runs entirely in your browser. There is no account to create, nothing to download, and nothing uploaded to a server · your subjects, grades, and plans stay on your device. Every calculation shows its working on the methodology page, so you can verify any number against your own institution's rules.
