AI Study Tools

Five AI workflows built on your notes and files · chat with your study materials, generate flashcards, build practice quizzes, draft a study plan from your syllabus tracker, and rewrite notes. Free tools stay free; this is an opt-in extra that uses your API key with the AI provider of your choice.

Before you use these tools, know this:
  • Your prompts go directly from this browser to the provider you choose. ExamElm AI is the exception: it goes through this site's own proxy to OpenRouter. Your prompts and your key are never seen by us.
  • The provider you choose receives everything you send, including your notes and files.
  • Nothing is sent anywhere until you press Send. Every tab shows you exactly what will be sent and an estimated cost first.
  • API billing is separate from any consumer AI subscription · you pay the provider's API rates.

What you can do here

  • Context Chat · ask questions answered only from the notes and files you select.
  • Flashcards · generate question/answer cards from a note or pasted text, review them, and save decks.
  • Quiz · generate multiple-choice and short-answer quizzes, take them, and track your scores.
  • Study Planner · draft a day-by-day plan from your syllabus tracker coverage, then open it in the timetable generator.
  • Notes Assistant · summarize, expand, simplify, or extract key points from any note, with preview before anything changes.
