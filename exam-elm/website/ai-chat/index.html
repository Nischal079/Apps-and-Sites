Before you connect a provider, know this

  • Your prompts go directly from this browser to the provider you choose. ExamElm AI is the exception: it goes through this site's own proxy to OpenRouter. We never see your key.
  • The provider you choose receives everything you send, including any text you paste.
  • Your API keys are stored encrypted in this browser. They are sent only to the provider you choose.
  • API billing is separate from ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini or any other consumer subscription · you pay the provider's API rates.
  • Nothing is sent anywhere until you press Send.
